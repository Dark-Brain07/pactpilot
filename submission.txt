#### PactPilot

**Title:** PactPilot - Autonomous Commercial Assurance & Obligation Monitoring Protocol
**Description:**
A GenLayer intelligent contract that transforms static agreements into living, monitored obligations with checkpoint schedules and approved public web sources. Counterparties bind themselves to exact immutable obligation digests before checkpoints begin. Independent validators independently fetch and interpret external evidence (using gl.nondet.web.get and LLM prompt execution) and reach consensus on semantic status (SATISFIED, AT_RISK, BREACHED, or UNRESOLVED) using GenLayer's comparative equivalence principle (gl.eq_principle.prompt_comparative).
